Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026.

What this app is

Snap Count is a free, unofficial fan game. It does not require an account and is not affiliated with any professional football league, team, or players association.

Data we store on your device

Game progress, settings, streaks, and achievements are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device only. That information never leaves your device unless you choose to share a score or screenshot yourself.

Data we do not collect

  • No sign-in, email, or profile
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers for sale
  • No official player photos or likeness uploads

Network use

The game loads its own files and a player roster snapshot from the site that hosts it (for example GitHub Pages). A service worker may cache those files so you can play offline. We do not run a separate analytics or advertising service in this version.

Player data sources

Roster facts come from nflverse (CC BY 4.0) and public depth or popularity hints from the Sleeper API. See the in-game About / How to play screen for attribution.

Children

The game is a sports trivia puzzle and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
