Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

What this app is

Snap Count is a free, unofficial fan game. It does not require an account and is not affiliated with any professional football league, team, or players association.

Data we store on your device

Game progress, settings, streaks, achievements, and a first-result lock for each daily puzzle are saved in your browser’s local storage (and a small IndexedDB backup) on this device. That gameplay progress stays on the device unless you choose to post a score or share a screenshot yourself.

Leaderboard posts

If you post a score, we send a nickname you choose, your guess count, a server-measured solve time, the puzzle date, and a random device identifier (not your name, email, or phone) to a hosted database so the score can appear on the public daily, weekly, and streak boards, or on a friend group you join. Nicknames are filtered for kid-safe language. Posting is optional. We do not require an account.

Visitor counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter, to see how many people play, which sites sent them (for example Reddit or X), and which country they visited from. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not store anything in your browser, does not store your IP address or a tracker ID, and does not sell data. Country is derived from a short-lived network lookup; your exact location is not recorded. When a daily puzzle ends we also send a simple “puzzle won” or “puzzle lost” event, with no player name and no guess details. You can read GoatCounter’s privacy policy at goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Data we do not collect

  • No sign-in, email, or profile
  • No precise (GPS) location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers for sale
  • No official player photos or likeness uploads

Network use

The game loads its own files and a player roster snapshot from the site that hosts it (for example GitHub Pages). A service worker may cache those files so you can play offline. Leaderboard reads and optional score posts go to our Supabase project. A small GoatCounter script is loaded from gc.zgo.at to count visits. We do not run a separate advertising service in this version.

Player data sources

Roster facts come from nflverse (CC BY 4.0) and public depth or popularity hints from the Sleeper API. See the in-game About / How to play screen for attribution.

Children

The game is a sports trivia puzzle and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
